# The Price of Hidden Sin

## Lesson Brief

- **Series:** Families in the Bible
- **Subject/Theme:** Achan and his household; the cost of hidden sin.
- **Bible Focus:** Achan disobeys God's command at Jericho, Israel suffers defeat at Ai, and God exposes the sin.
- **Main Passage:** [Joshua 7](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/Joshua-Chapter-7/). Background: [Joshua 1:1-9](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/Joshua-Chapter-1/) and [Joshua 6](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/Joshua-Chapter-6/), especially verses 17-19.
- **Main Point:** Our sin can bring great harm to the people around us, so we must turn from it and live honestly before God.
- **Teaching Aim:** Call children to confess hidden sin, seek help to make wrongs right, and obey God's Word.
- **Teacher Preparation:** Keep the conquest background brief. Give the most time to Achan's choices and their cost to his household and Israel. Read the judgment calmly, without graphic detail.

## Memory Verse

**Proverbs 28:13 (KJV):** “He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.” Explain *confess* as telling the truth about our sin and *forsake* as turning away from it. Recommended for the lesson's call to action.

## Lesson Outline

### 1. God Gives Israel Victory at Jericho

**Give a Brief  History**: 

- Egypt
- Exodus
- Wilderness


**Summary/Lesson:** After Moses died, Joshua led the people of Israel into the Promised Land. God told Joshua to follow His Word as he led the people. God was going to give them the land, but they would have to overcome many enemies first. 

At Jericho, a mighty city with great walls, God gave unusual instructions. The people marched around the city once a day for six days. On the seventh day, they went around it seven times. When they were done, the priests blew the trumpets and the people shouted. The wall fell, and Israel entered the city. God had given them the victory. But He still had certain instructions for them to follow. 

<image: Joshua standing over the rubble of Jericho with the israelite army behind him, them all cheering>

### 2. God Warns Them About Jericho's Spoils

> **Joshua 6:17-19**
> And the city shall be accursed, even it, and all that are therein, to the LORD: only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers that we sent. And ye, in any wise keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed, when ye take of the accursed thing, and make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it. But all the silver, and gold, and vessels of brass and iron, are consecrated unto the LORD: they shall come into the treasury of the LORD.

**Summary/Lesson:**  Spoils are the goods taken after a battle. Jericho was under God's judgment. Rahab and those with her in her house were to be spared. The city and its goods were to be destroyed while the silver, gold, and vessels of brass and iron were to go into the Lord's treasury.

Joshua warned that taking the forbidden things would bring trouble on Israel's camp. The people had been told what to do and warned about the harm disobedience would bring. 

So no matter what they saw or wanted when they went in the city, were they allowed to take any of it? No, it all belonged to God

### 3. Achan Wants What God Has Forbidden

> **Joshua 7:1**
> But the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing: for Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing: and the anger of the LORD was kindled against the children of Israel.

> **Joshua 7:20-21**
> And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the LORD God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done: When I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight, then I coveted them, and took them; and, behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it.

**Summary/Lesson:** A man named Achan saw some very nice clothes, and a lot of silver and gold. The Bible says that when he saw them, he coveted them, which means he wanted them very badly for himself. Now boys and girls, is that something that he was allowed to have? No. God said that those things were for the Lord. 

<image: Achan taking some nice clothes, silver, and gold from the rubble, looking around making sure no one is watch, a look of worry on his face>

But Achan took them and hid them under the ground inside his tent. He had chosen to disobey God, then hide what he did. He already knew what he was doing was wrong, otherwise he wouldn't have hid it. 

> **Hebrews 4:13**
> Neither is there any creature that is not manifest in his sight: but all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do.

This tells us that God sees everything. It doesn't matter where we are or what we're doing. God can see us and He knows us. He knows our hearts. He knows our minds, and He sees what we are doing, even when we try to hide from Him. Achan couldn't hide from God any more than Jonah could. 

### 4. Israel Suffers a Great Loss at Ai

The next battle after Jericho was a small city called Ai. Joshua sent spies to Ai, just like he did to Jericho. But when the spies came back, they said it was a small city and it wouldn't be a very hard battle, so they shouldn't send very many people to fight. So Joshua took their advice and only sent a small army to the battle:

> **Joshua 7:4-5**
> So there went up thither of the people about three thousand men: and they fled before the men of Ai. And the men of Ai smote of them about thirty and six men: for they chased them from before the gate even unto Shebarim, and smote them in the going down: wherefore the hearts of the people melted, and became as water.

<image: the israelite army fleeing a small city of Ai (as described by the Bible) with fear on their faces>

**Summary/Lesson:** Even though it was a small city, AI won the battle and 36 Israelites were killed. This wasn't supposed to happen. Israel's army was much bigger than AI's, and they should have easily overtaken the city. 

But think about this: those soldiers were people. They were husbands, fathers, sons. Families lost loved ones. Little children would never see their daddies again, Wives wouldn't see their husbands, parents wouldn't see their sons! Joshua mourned before the Lord.

> **Proverbs 28:13**
> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.

Who was covering their sin?

### 5. God Tells Joshua There Is Sin in the Camp

> **Joshua 7:10-13**
> And the LORD said unto Joshua, Get thee up; wherefore liest thou thus upon thy face? Israel hath sinned, and they have also transgressed my covenant which I commanded them: for they have even taken of the accursed thing, and have also stolen, and dissembled also, and they have put it even among their own stuff. Therefore the children of Israel could not stand before their enemies, but turned their backs before their enemies, because they were accursed: neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from among you. Up, sanctify the people, and say, Sanctify yourselves against to morrow: for thus saith the LORD God of Israel, There is an accursed thing in the midst of thee, O Israel: thou canst not stand before thine enemies, until ye take away the accursed thing from among you.

> **Joshua 7:15**
> And it shall be, that he that is taken with the accursed thing shall be burnt with fire, he and all that he hath: because he hath transgressed the covenant of the LORD, and because he hath wrought folly in Israel.

<image: early in the morning, joshua is on his knees praying to God>

**Summary/Lesson:** God told Joshua to get up. Israel had sinned and broken His command. What was the command? For them to not take anything that they wanted from Jericho. But they had. The stole from God and hid the truth. God told Joshua that Israel could not stand before its enemies or that He would no longer be with them while this remained among them.

The people were to prepare for the next morning. God gave instructions for Joshua to go tribe by tribe until God pointed out the right tribe, then family by family, then house by house, until God pointed out the person that was sinning against God and all of Israel.

### 6. The Search Reaches Achan

So in the morning, All the people got up and stood in huge groups broken up by tribe, by families, by leaders, by households. The Joshua started walking by each tribe:

- Tribes: Reuben, Manasseh, Simeon, Dan, Judah - "STOP" God stays
- Families: Stephanites, Wattanaparudites, Waterhites, Bockhoffites, Bradfordites, Meltonites, Zarhites - "STOP"
- Household: Zabdi
- Achan

<image: throngs of people surrounding Joshua, as he points to Achan with dread on his face. Joshua is point with a great deal of sadness, but the firm stand of a leader.>

> **Joshua 7:19-23**
> And Joshua said unto Achan, My son, give, I pray thee, glory to the LORD God of Israel, and make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me. And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the LORD God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done: When I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight, then I coveted them, and took them; and, behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it. So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent; and, behold, it was hid in his tent, and the silver under it. And they took them out of the midst of the tent, and brought them unto Joshua, and unto all the children of Israel, and laid them out before the LORD.

**Summary/Lesson:** Achan was found out. There was no more hiding, no more secrets. And not only was he found out, but the entire nation of Israel was there watching. They all saw him: his family, his friends, his neighbors, his boss, his coworkers, his enemies, people he never met before, all saw him as a thief, a liar, the reason their father, husband, or son died in the battle.

Joshua urged Achan to confess what he had done. Achan then admitted that he had sinned against the Lord. He told about the clothes, silver, and gold and where he had buried them. Messengers ran to the tent and found them there. Everything he had hidden was brought out before the Lord. 

Boys and girls, it is so important to confess and admit your sins before you get caught. Things will always be better if you tell your parents you did something naughty before they catch you. Just the same, it is always better if you confess to God before He punishes you for your sin.

> **Proverbs 15:3**
> The eyes of the LORD are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.

### 7. His Household Shares the Cost

> **Joshua 7:24-26**
> And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor. And Joshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? the LORD shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones. And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the LORD turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of Achor, unto this day.

**Summary/Lesson:** Achan was brought to the valley of Achor. But not just him, his sons and daughters were brought with him, along with his animals, tent, possessions, and the stolen goods. God's judgment fell. Achan was stoned, so were his animals, and his belongings, and his children. Can you imagine? His children who had no part in his sin, were killed because of his sin. Your sin doesn't affect just you. Others pay for your sin too. A great heap of stones remained at that place. The Lord then turned from His fierce anger.

<image: Achan and his family are behind the camera, but we can see the anger in the people's eyes as they are throwing stone at and over the camera. It's a wide angle view>

No only did 36 men loose their lives in the battle, but Achan's family all died as well. God is serious about us obeying His commands. We cannot expect to disobey Got and be able to do whatever we want. We have to do right.

### 8. Live Honestly and Keep Your Heart Before God

**Summary/Lesson:** Achan hid his sin until God pointed him out to Joshua. God calls us to confess our sin and turn away from it. When we repent, God shows forgiveness:

> **Proverbs 28:13**
> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.

- Invite each child to consider a wrong they need to bring to God. 
- Invite them to pray and confess their sin to God and ask for forgiveness
-  Tell them to avoid hiding things when they sin and to make it right with their parents and God right away.

## Segue Into The Plan Of Salvation

Achan's story shows the serious cost of sin. We also need forgiveness for our own sin. God knows what each of us has done, including the wrong we have tried to hide.

Jesus Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again the third day:

> **1 Corinthians 15:3-4**
> For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:

He is the Saviour we need. Ephesians 2:8-9 teaches that salvation is God's gift, received through faith. Good conduct cannot earn it.

Acts 16:31 calls us to believe on the Lord Jesus Christ:

> **Acts 16:31**
> And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.

Invite children who have questions about trusting Him to speak with a teacher. Give them space to ask and understand.

## Review Questions and Answers

1. **Who led Israel into the Promised Land after Moses died?**  
   **Answer:** Joshua.
2. **What city did Israel march around before its walls fell?**  
   **Answer:** Jericho.
3. **How many times did Israel march around Jericho on the seventh day?**  
   **Answer:** Seven times.
4. **Who gave Israel the victory at Jericho?**  
   **Answer:** God.
5. **What are spoils?**  
   **Answer:** Goods taken after a battle.
6. **Where were Jericho's silver, gold, and vessels of brass and iron supposed to go?**  
   **Answer:** Into the Lord's treasury.
7. **What did Joshua warn would happen if someone took the forbidden things?**  
   **Answer:** It would bring trouble on Israel's camp.
8. **Who took things that God had forbidden the people to take?**  
   **Answer:** Achan.
9. **What did Achan take?**  
   **Answer:** A Babylonian garment, silver, and a wedge of gold.
10. **What does it mean that Achan coveted those things?**  
    **Answer:** He wanted them for himself even though God had forbidden him to take them.
11. **Where did Achan hide the stolen things?**  
    **Answer:** In the ground inside his tent.
12. **Could Achan hide what he had done from God?**  
    **Answer:** No. God sees everything and knows our hearts.
13. **What city defeated Israel after the victory at Jericho?**  
    **Answer:** Ai.
14. **About how many Israelites died in that battle?**  
    **Answer:** Thirty-six.
15. **What did God tell Joshua was wrong in the camp?**  
    **Answer:** Israel had sinned by taking forbidden things and hiding them.
16. **What did Achan do when Joshua asked him to tell what he had done?**  
    **Answer:** He admitted that he had sinned and told Joshua what he had taken and where it was hidden.
17. **Who suffered because of Achan's sin?**  
    **Answer:** Achan, his household, and the nation of Israel. His choice brought harm to many people.
18. **According to Proverbs 28:13, what should we do with our sins?**  
    **Answer:** Confess them and forsake them. That means telling the truth about our sin and turning away from it.
19. **You have done something wrong that your parents have not discovered. What should you do?**  
    **Answer:** Confess it to God, tell your parents the truth, and ask for help to make it right.
20. **Who can save us from our sins, and what did He do for us?**  
    **Answer:** Jesus Christ. He died for our sins, was buried, and rose again the third day.
