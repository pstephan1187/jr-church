# The Price of Hidden Sin — Lesson Image Plan

- **Passage:** Joshua 6–7.
- **Style:** Parent series-image-style.md and approved cover-graphic.png. Polished painted storybook art, dimensional cartoon forms, warm earth and sky colors, clear expressions. All scenes are 16:9 PNGs without text or logos.
- **Achan:** Preserve the approved cover identity: olive-brown complexion, lean build, receding wavy black hair graying at the temples, long narrow salt-and-pepper beard, blue patterned mantle and cream tunic.
- **Joshua:** New artistic design, distinct from Achan: older stocky man, broad square face, medium-brown complexion, bald crown with straight shoulder-length silver side hair, broad full silver beard, royal red mantle and red tunic with gold borders, rust belt, small ivory neckline underlayer, and leather sandals. He holds an ancient bronze sword at his side in image 1. Carry this identity through images 1, 4, and 5.
- **Other people:** Ancient Israelites and Canaanites with varied individual faces, natural olive to medium-brown complexions, woven garments and historically suitable materials. Image 3 foreground designs: older lean man with a long face and pointed graying beard at left; young round-faced man with light stubble in the center; stockier square-faced man with a short square beard at right.
- **Illustration details:** Facial expressions, poses, clothing, and layouts are artistic choices. Image 4 follows the user's requested dawn setting as a teaching illustration; Joshua 7:6 describes his prayer continuing until evening. Image 6 uses an imagined wide camera position, with all victims offscreen and no impact, injury, or gore shown.
