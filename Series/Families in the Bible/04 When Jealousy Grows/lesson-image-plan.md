# When Jealousy Grows Image Plan

- **Series:** Families in the Bible
- **Lesson:** When Jealousy Grows
- **Passage:** Genesis 4:1-16
- **Style and character source:** Approved `cover-graphic.png` and parent `series-image-style.md`.
- **Scope:** Six images, one for each `<image: ...>` tag in document order. Replace each tag in place with a relative Markdown image.
- **Continuity:** Keep the cover's warm olive/tan complexions, individual faces, and rough animal-skin garments. Abel is the lean young adult with shorter brown hair and light beard, cream hide across his chest, and shepherd's staff. Cain is the stockier young adult with a fuller dark beard and dark brown hide vest. Adam has a bald crown, gray side hair, and a full squared gray-brown beard. Eve has a fuller face and brown hair pulled into a braid. Their home is a primitive hide-and-branch shelter.
- **Tone:** Painted storybook art, clear adult faces and actions, restrained emotion, no logo or text. Grave markers are rough unmarked natural stones. No visible wounds, bodies, weapons, or blood.
- **Passage care:** The work details, altars, possible right response, grave markers, and family farewell are teaching illustrations. Do not present those details as events recorded in Genesis. Do not show a visible figure of God or a supernatural sign of accepted offerings.

1. `image-1.png` — Point 1. Split image: Cain kneels to plant a seed in cultivated soil; Abel gently tends a sheep with a sore leg. Show different work and skills. Keep injury concealed and mild.
2. `image-2.png` — Point 2. Both brothers bow as they place their offerings on simple rough stone altars. Cain brings produce; Abel brings an offering from his flock. Keep the animal offering partly concealed and non-graphic.
3. `image-3.png` — Point 3. Cain stands in his garden in the foreground, watching Abel with his sheep in the distance, visibly angry.
4. `image-4.png` — Point 4. Split teaching illustration: Cain happily brings another offering as an imagined response to correction; the other half shows Cain sobbing beneath a dark sky on barren land. Same man and clothes on both sides.
5. `image-5.png` — Point 5. A simple rough unmarked grave stone in a field, showing the loss of Abel's life without depicting violence. Symbolic illustration.
6. `image-6.png` — Point 6. Cain walks away dejected while Adam and Eve kneel and weep beside the same grave stone. Imagined family scene showing grief; no body or violence.
