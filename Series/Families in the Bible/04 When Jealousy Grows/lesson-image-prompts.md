# When Jealousy Grows Image Prompts

- **Tool:** Built-in imagegen, one generation per image.
- **Output:** Six PNG images in this folder, inserted at their source tags in `lesson-outline.md`.
- **Prompt assembly:** Each submitted prompt is the shared prompt below, two newline characters, then its numbered scene prompt. References are listed for each scene.

## Shared Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create a 16:9 landscape PNG, ideally 1672 x 941, for the Junior Church lesson "When Jealousy Grows", Families in the Bible, Genesis 4.
REFERENCE: supplied approved lesson cover is the exact CHARACTER IDENTITY and painting-style anchor. Match this specific family; do not invent substitute faces. Remove all cover lettering and logo from scene art. Polished painted children's storybook illustration with dimensional cartoon adults, expressive natural faces, fine painted hair and hide textures, warm golden sunshine, cream, olive, sky-blue, terracotta and deep navy shading. Preserve the exact LIGHT-TO-MEDIUM WARM OLIVE/TAN complexion range of the cover.
CHARACTER LOCK: Abel is the LEFT young man in the cover: lean adult about 25, longer oval face, short loosely wavy dark brown hair and short light beard, cream fur/hide sash diagonally over a brown animal-skin tunic, rope belt. Cain is the FAR RIGHT young man in the cover: stockier adult about 28, broad angular jaw and darker full short beard, short low-volume dark brown hair, dark brown furry hide vest/tunic and hide belt. Do not swap the brothers, costumes or complexion. Adam is the tall sturdy father with bald crown, short gray-brown hair at sides, squared gray-brown beard, brown-and-cream hide tunic. Eve is the mother with a fuller mature face, brown hair gathered in a braid, cream-and-brown animal-skin dress. All clothing modest, rough fur-and-hide, no modern clothes or ornaments.
ENVIRONMENT: earliest-family rural landscape outside Eden, simple hide-and-branch homestead when needed, unpaved soil, grass, stones, plants and rolling hills. No town or finished masonry.
CONSTRAINTS: entire canvas is illustration with no titles, logo, caption, words, letters, labels, watermarks or borders. No modern objects, polished cemetery headstones, crosses, visible wounds, blood, violence, weapons, scary faces, ghostly figures or a visible depiction of God. Familiar painted style for children ages 4-12.
```
