- **Series:** Families in the Bible

- **Continuity:** Jochebed wears terracotta and a cream scarf; Miriam wears teal-blue with a sand sash; infant Moses has cream swaddling with a blue edge. Amram wears tan and brown; young Aaron wears olive green.
