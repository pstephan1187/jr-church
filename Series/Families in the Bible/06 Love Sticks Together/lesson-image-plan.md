# Love Sticks Together Lesson Image Plan

- **Passages:** [Ruth 1-4, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ruth+1-4&version=KJV); Matthew 1:5-17; [Luke 2:1-20, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+2%3A1-20&version=KJV).
- **Count:** Nine source tags in document order. The wedding and baby tag produces one split image.
- **Style:** `../series-image-style.md` and the approved `cover-graphic.png`. Painted storybook art, warm colors, 1672 × 941 PNG. Ordinary scene images have no lettering or logo.
- **Character identity:** Naomi and Ruth follow the approved cover. In image 1 Naomi appears about ten years younger with the same facial structure and clothing colors. Elimelech has an oblong square face and receding grey-dark waves; the adult sons differ in face, build, hair and beard. Orpah has a longer rectangular face, softly squared jaw and gently cleft chin, smaller almond-shaped eyes, straighter brows, a softly broad straight nose, and smooth center-parted black hair with a heavy braid. Her warm olive complexion, ochre scarf, sage tunic and rust-brown wrap stay consistent in images 2 and 3. Boaz has a broad square face, curly dark/grey hair, a neat full beard, rust-red mantle, cream tunic and blue sash. David's adult design extends the boy at the far right of lesson 02's cover. Mary and Joseph have their own faces and simple blue/cream and sage/beige clothes. Ages and appearance are artistic choices.
