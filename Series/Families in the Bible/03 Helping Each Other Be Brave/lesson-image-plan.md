# Helping Each Other Be Brave Image Plan

- **Series:** Families in the Bible
- **Lesson:** Helping Each Other Be Brave
- **Passage:** Esther 2–9
- **Style source:** `../series-image-style.md` and `cover-graphic.png`
- **Count:** Seven images, one for each existing `<image:…>` tag, in document order.
- **Continuity:** Match the cover’s warm painted storybook style. Adult Esther keeps her dark hair, purple-and-cream gown and diadem. Mordecai matches the cover’s face, graying beard and modest worker clothing before his later honor; image 1 shows a younger version. King Ahasuerus and Haman keep distinct, consistent faces and outfits. No logos or embedded text.
- **Illustration boundary:** The cemetery and infant age, carried procession, written message, and additional scene participants are requested illustrative staging rather than claims of exact details recorded in Scripture. Palace settings are Persian.

1. `image-1.png` — Mordecai as a young adult gently holding baby Esther in a quiet ancient cemetery. A tender illustration of his care after her parents died; the Bible does not specify her age or this cemetery scene.
2. `image-2.png` — Young adult Esther stands respectfully before King Ahasuerus in his Persian throne room, with royal advisors nearby, as she becomes queen.
3. `image-3.png` — Haman travels through the Persian city on a dignitary’s carried litter while townspeople bow and Mordecai stands upright. The procession staging illustrates the requested scene; Scripture records the refusal to bow.
4. `image-4.png` — Split composition: Esther reads a message handed to her by the messenger inside the palace, visibly concerned; outside the palace, Mordecai speaks firmly but lovingly to the same messenger. Mordecai wears the same cream tunic, blue-gray robe with narrow patterned borders, and brown sash as in image 3, matching the teacher’s requested clothing continuity.
5. `image-5.png` — View from behind Esther at the entrance to the royal inner court, looking toward the seated king consulting Haman. Communicate Esther’s courageous approach; Haman’s presence is the requested illustration staging.
6. `image-6.png` — At the second banquet, Esther points firmly toward Haman as she names the enemy. The king looks surprised and Haman frightened. A small number of attendants dine farther back, as requested, without distracting from the main three figures.
7. `image-7.png` — Jewish families celebrate their rescue in the Persian capital. Esther and Mordecai are visible together; Mordecai now wears the blue-and-white royal apparel, purple linen mantle, and gold crown of his later honor (Esther 8:15).
