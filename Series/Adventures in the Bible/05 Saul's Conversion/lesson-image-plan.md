# The Hunter Jesus Hunted Down Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** The Hunter Jesus Hunted Down
- **Passage:** Acts 7:54-60; Acts 8:1-3; Acts 9:1-22
- **Style source:** `../series-image-style.md` and `cover-graphic.png`

1. `image-1.png` - Introduction: the joyful early church grows as believers share the gospel in Jerusalem, while Saul watches from the edge with rising hostility; establishes what Saul opposed.
2. `image-2.png` - Point 1: Stephen kneels outside Jerusalem looking heavenward while an angry crowd prepares to stone him; Saul stands apart with folded arms, calmly approving; no discarded clothes, impact, or gore are shown.
3. `image-3.png` - Point 2: Saul and temple officers enter a Christian family's home and take the parents toward prison while frightened children remain close; communicates the real harm of Saul's house-to-house persecution without physical violence.
4. `image-4.png` - Point 3: Saul strides down the long road to Damascus carrying a sealed authorization scroll, with companions behind him and the distant city ahead; his determined hunt is about to meet an unexpected end.
5. `image-5.png` - Point 4 and climax: brilliant light from heaven stops Saul near Damascus; he falls on the road shielding his eyes while his companions recoil; Jesus is represented by light only.
6. `image-6.png` - Point 5: inside the Damascus house, Ananias gently lays his hands on the blind, humbled Saul and welcomes him as "Brother Saul"; the mood turns warm, merciful, and hopeful.
7. `image-7.png` - Point 6: the transformed Saul boldly preaches that Jesus is the Son of God in a synagogue; listeners react with amazement while Damascus believers recognize the former persecutor's changed direction.
