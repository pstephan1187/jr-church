# A Tower to Heaven Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** A Tower to Heaven
- **Passage:** Genesis 9:1, 7; Genesis 10:8-10; Genesis 11:1-9
- **Style source:** `Series/Adventures in the Bible/series-image-style.md`, the series cover, and Lesson 8 scene images

1. `image-1.png` - After the flood, Noah's family and their growing descendants spread across a bright open landscape, showing God's command to fill the earth.
2. `image-2.png` - Nimrod and the people settle together on the plain of Shinar, with one gathered community choosing to remain in one place instead of spreading out.
3. `image-3.png` - Nimrod leads a proud building project as workers make bricks and raise a huge unfinished tower, showing the desire for fame and human achievement.
4. `image-4.png` - A high, wide view shows the LORD examining the city and tower while the small builders continue their proud work below, showing that God sees rebellion and hidden thoughts.
5. `image-5.png` - The builders become confused when they cannot understand each other, and families leave the unfinished tower in different directions, showing the cost of judgment and scattering.
6. `image-6.png` - The unfinished tower stops below the bright sky while scattered bricks sit in the foreground and a humble child looks toward a simple cross on a distant hill, showing that human effort cannot bring a sinner to God and that salvation comes through Jesus.
