# The Runaway Prophet Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** The Runaway Prophet
- **Passage:** Jonah 1-3
- **Style source:** `series-image-style.md` and `cover-graphic.png`

1. `image-1.png` - God speaks to Jonah inside his Israelite home; warm heavenly light fills the room as Jonah listens with sober concern.
2. `image-2.png` - Nineveh at night is filled with dishonesty and cruelty as people cheat, steal, argue, and mistreat a poor family.
3. `image-3.png` - Jonah chooses the opposite direction, walking down to the harbor at Joppa and boarding a wooden ship bound for Tarshish.
4. `image-4.png` - A mighty but child-safe sea storm shakes Jonah’s ship as worried sailors work on deck and Jonah sleeps below.
5. `image-5.png` - From deep beneath the calm sea, Jonah sinks helplessly below the visible underside of the ship and reaches toward the distant surface.
6. `image-6.png` - Jonah is cramped inside the great fish, struggling in misery among slime, seaweed, shells, and half-digested seafood.
7. `image-7.png` - Still coated in seafood residue with frayed clothes and ragged hair, Jonah warns Nineveh as its people and king turn humbly to God.
