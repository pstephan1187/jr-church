# The Runaway Prophet Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** The Runaway Prophet
- **Passage:** Jonah 1-3
- **Style source:** `series-image-style.md` and `cover-graphic.png`

1. `image-1.png` - God calls Jonah, an Israelite prophet, to carry His warning to the great Assyrian city of Nineveh; Jonah looks troubled as Nineveh appears far beyond the eastern hills.
2. `image-2.png` - Jonah receives God’s clear direction toward Nineveh; a bright journey path leads east while Jonah understands the command.
3. `image-3.png` - Jonah chooses the opposite direction, walking down to the harbor at Joppa and boarding a wooden ship bound for Tarshish.
4. `image-4.png` - A mighty but child-safe sea storm shakes Jonah’s ship as worried sailors work on deck and Jonah sleeps below.
5. `image-5.png` - The sailors place Jonah into the sea; the storm stops at once and the sea becomes calm, showing the serious consequence of rebellion.
6. `image-6.png` - God’s great fish carries Jonah toward shore; a gentle storybook cutaway shows Jonah praying inside its softly lit belly.
7. `image-7.png` - Jonah faithfully warns Nineveh and its people, including the king, turn from evil and pray humbly to God.
