# Going on Your own Adventures

## Lesson Brief

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Subject/Theme:** The Christian life is an adventure with real struggles. God gives every believer what is needed to stand against the enemy and live faithfully.
- **Bible Focus:** The believer's spiritual battle, the guidance of God's Word, training from faithful teachers, the whole armor of God, and victory through Jesus Christ.
- **Main Passage:** Ephesians 6:10-18
- **Supporting Passages:** Psalm 119:105; 1 Peter 5:8-9; 2 Timothy 3:14-17; James 4:7
- **Main Point:** God gives His children His Word, faithful teachers, spiritual training, His armor, and His strength so they can stand against the enemy.
- **Lesson Goal:** Help the children recognize common spiritual attacks and choose to use the help that God provides.

## Memory Verse

> **Ephesians 6:11**
>
> Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

## Lesson Introduction

For several weeks, we have heard about adventures in the Bible. Ehud faced a cruel king. Jael faced an enemy commander. The disciples crossed a stormy sea. Paul escaped over a city wall in a basket. Jonah spent three days inside a great fish. Elisha woke up surrounded by an army.

Those adventures happened to real people. Each person had to decide whether to trust God and obey Him or to do their own thing.

Your life may not include a palace, a giant fish, some great escape, or an army of horses and chariots, but you still have an adventure ahead of you. God has a purpose for your life. You will face challenges, hateful people, and hard choices. Satan will push you toward sin. You will meet fears and temptations. You will have chances to serve God, tell others about Jesus, and stand for what is right.

The Christian life is a great adventure because, just like any other adventurer, we have a higher purpose. Any time someone goes to battle, or puts their life on the line, they do it for a purpose greater than themselves. There is something they love that they are fighting for. Maybe it's their:

- Family
- Friends
- Country
- The truth
- Freedom

People have given up their health, their money, even their lives for great purposes. As Christians, we have the greatest purpose of all: to serve God. And He gives us everything we need to succeed.

## Lesson Outline

### 1. The Christian Life Includes A Real Battle

> **Ephesians 6:10-12**
>
> Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

[ [Image]: a you boy practicing with a teacher in an archery range ]

**Summary/Lesson:** Paul wrote these words to Christians. He told them to be strong in the Lord. The strength for the Christian life comes from God. Without His strength, we would fail! A weak person does not do well in an adventure. Adventures require string, preparation, and wisdom.

Paul also warned them about the devil's “wiles.” A wile is a trick or a sneaky plan. Satan is very sneaky. He is very good and making people thing that sin is ok or even good! Satan is God's enemy. He hates what God loves. He uses lies, temptation, fear, and false teaching to pull people away from God.

Our main enemies are spiritual. The person who bothers you at school is a person whom God loves. Your brother or sister is not the true enemy. People may do wrong things, and their actions may need to be stopped. But our true enemy is wickeness, pride, rejection of God, temptation to do wrong. That is what we are fighting against:

> **Ephesians 6:12**
> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

As we go through our life, we are going to face A lot of these enemies, sometimes they're not going to look like evil and sin and wickedness. Sometimes they're just going to look like fun. Sometimes they're gonna look good. Sometimes they're gonna be things. That are hard to know if they're right or wrong. We need to remember that the devil is very sneaky and he wants to trick us into doing wrong and he lays traps for us everywhere. 

So how do you win this battle? How do you come home from this adventure victorious? Just like any adventurer or warrior, you prepare. 

### 2. Learn To See the Dangers

> **1 Peter 5:8-9**
>
> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: Whom resist stedfast in the faith.

[ [Image]: a child walking through a path in the woods, traps everywhere, and a personified lion hiding behind a tree, ready to pounce ]

**Summary/Lesson:** Satan has many ways to attack Christians. Sometimes he doesn't attack at all, sometimes he just lays traps and hopes we fall for them.

Here are some attacks a child may face:

- **Temptation to hide sin:** “Tell a lie so you will not get in trouble.”
- **Pressure from friends:** “Everyone else watches it, says it, or does it. You should join us.”
- **Distraction:** “You have plenty of time for games and videos. The Bible and prayer can wait.”
- **Victimhood**: "You didn't get what you deserved, you should feel sorry for yourself"
- **Doubt:** “God does not care about you. He will not help you.”
- **Fear:** “Stay quiet about Jesus. People may laugh at you.”
- **Pride:** “You know better than your parents, pastor, or teacher.”  or "No one understands or cares what I'm going through!"
- **Discouragement:** “You failed again. You might as well stop trying to do right.”
- **Bitterness:** “That person hurt you. They don't deserve to be forgiven.”
- **Shame after sin:** “You have done something very bad. God will not forgive you, you might as well keep sinning.”

Satan will use simple things to get to you just a little bit. But the little things add up, and before you know it, you aren't going to church anymore, you're not obeying God or your parents, you have a bad attitude, etc. That's why you have to stop the little things.

### 3. Follow the guide

> **Psalm 119:105**
>
> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

[ [Image]: a child walking down a dangerous dark path with a map and a flashlight ]

**Summary/Lesson:** An explorer needs a trustworthy guide. Adventures study everything they can about where they are going. They read maps, they learn the language, they study the culture, they find out about the dangers in the area, they hire a local to lead them.

Christians need to do the same thing! God's Word is the guide for our Christian adventure. A lamp gives enough light for the next step. God's Word guides us as we keep walking. It shows us the dangers, it leads us through safe paths. We can follow it to keep us safe!

The Bible tells us what is right and wrong. It warns us about danger. It teaches us how to be saved. It gives examples of people who trusted God and people who ignored Him. It also gives promises that we can remember when we are afraid.

Imagine that a friend asks you to help hide something from a teacher. Your feelings may say, “Help your friend.” Fear may say, “He will stop liking you if you tell the truth.” God's Word gives clear light:

> **Ephesians 4:25**
>
> Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour.

The Bible helps us see the next right step. We must read it, listen when it is taught, learn what it means, memorize it, and obey it.

**Practical Application for Children:** Read a small part of the Bible each day. Ask two questions: “What does this teach me?” and “What should I do?”

### 4. God Gives Us Teachers And Training

> **2 Timothy 3:14-15**
>
> But continue thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them; And that from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.

[ [Image]: a boy practicing martials arts in a dojo with his sensei ]

**Summary/Lesson:** Adventurers spend years practicing, learning, and studying. Warriors spend years learning how to fight, use their weapons, and defend against the enemy. Christians should too! Timothy learned the Scriptures while he was a child. His mother and his grandmother taught him the Bible. Paul also trained Timothy and showed him how to serve God. Paul, John the Baptist, even Jesus, all spent long amounts of time studying, learning, and preparing to do what God wanted them to do.

God places faithful people in our lives to teach, warn, encourage, and correct us. Parents,  Pastors and Sunday school teachers, older Christians are all their to help you learn and understand more of the Bible and God's ways.

Training includes correction. A coach may stop an athlete and show him how to move correctly. A loving parent or teacher may stop a child who is moving toward sin. Correction can feel uncomfortable, but it can protect us from greater harm.

> **Proverbs 1:8**
>
> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother.

### 5. Wear Protection

Every warrior and adventurer wears some kind of protection. Whether it's against enemy soldiers, or just bugs and poison ivy, we must be protected from danger! Christians need protection too!

> **Ephesians 6:13**
>
> Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

[ [Image]: a boy putting on battle armor ]

A soldier needs every piece of his armor for battle. God tells Christians to put on the whole armor of God. Each piece has a specific purpose, a particular way it protects the Christian from harm.

> **Ephesians 6:14-17**
> Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace; Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:

Each piece is there to protect you a certain way and every piece is very important. If you leave a single piece off, you become much easier to hurt.

## Segue Into The Plan Of Salvation

The armor of God is for people who belong to God. A sinner becomes part of God's family through faith in Jesus Christ. The Christian adventure begins with salvation.

Every person has sinned.

> **Romans 3:23**
>
> For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.

Sin separates us from God and earns death.

> **Romans 6:23**
>
> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Jesus Christ, the Son of God, lived without sin. He died on the cross and took the punishment for our sins. He was buried, and He rose again on the third day. He won the greatest victory.

Salvation is God's gift. It comes by grace through faith in Jesus.

> **Ephesians 2:8-9**
>
> For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.

A child can admit that he is a sinner, believe that Jesus died and rose again, and call on the Lord to save him.

> **Romans 10:13**
>
> For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

Invite any child who has questions about salvation to speak with a teacher after the lesson. Explain the gospel from Scripture. Help the child place his faith in Jesus rather than in a prayer, a good deed, or a church activity.

## Main Applications For The Kids

- Recognize that Satan uses lies and temptation to pull people away from God.
- Use the Bible as the guide for each choice.
- Receive training and correction from faithful parents, teachers, pastors, and mature Christians.
- Put on the whole armor of God each day.
- Pray and ask other believers for help.
- Stand in God's strength and return to Him quickly when you sin.

## Closing Challenge

Your adventure with God may lead you into a classroom, a team, a hard friendship, a future job, or a place where few people know Jesus. You cannot see every challenge ahead. God sees it all.

He has given you a guide in His Word. He has placed teachers in your life. He gives you places to train. He offers the whole armor of God. 

Choose today to use what God provides. Follow His Word one step at a time. Stand in His strength. Through Jesus Christ, you can live faithfully on the adventure God gives you.
