# Going on Your Own Adventures Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** Going on Your Own Adventures
- **Passage:** Ephesians 6:10-18
- **Style source:** `series-image-style.md`, the series cover, and the existing lesson art
- **Continuity:** Use the same young boy throughout: about ten years old, warm tan skin, wavy medium-brown hair, brown eyes, and a brave but teachable expression. Keep the blue, gold, parchment, green, and warm-red series palette.

1. `image-1.png` - The boy practices archery at an outdoor range while a kind adult teacher corrects his stance. This introduces preparation and strength for a real battle.
2. `image-2.png` - The boy walks through a forest path filled with hidden snares and false paths while a stylized lion waits behind a tree. This shows Satan's traps in a clear, child-safe way.
3. `image-3.png` - At twilight, the boy follows a dangerous path with a parchment map in one hand and a bright flashlight in the other. This shows God's Word guiding each step.
4. `image-4.png` - The same boy practices martial arts in a bright dojo while a wise teacher guides his form. This shows patient training and correction.
5. `image-5.png` - The boy puts on bright, complete battle armor while a trusted adult helps fasten one piece. Every part of the armor is visible. This shows God's full protection.
