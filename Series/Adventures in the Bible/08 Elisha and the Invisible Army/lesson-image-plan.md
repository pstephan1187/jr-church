# Elisha and the Invisible Army Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** Elisha and the Invisible Army
- **Passage:** 2 Kings 6:8-23
- **Main point:** God gives us spiritual leaders who use His Word to warn, guide, and protect us, so we should listen with a teachable heart.
- **Style source:** `Series/Adventures in the Bible/series-image-style.md`, series cover, and nearby lesson images

1. `image-1.png` - The Syrian king secretly chooses an ambush site while soldiers hide among rocky hills; establish the unseen danger and God’s knowledge of the secret.
2. `image-2.png` - Elisha’s warning reaches Israel’s king, who listens in a palace courtyard while the hidden Syrian camp remains visible in the distant valley; show a teachable response to danger he cannot see.
3. `image-3.png` - Syrian horses, chariots, and a great host surround Dothan at night; show the faithful prophet trapped by an overwhelming force.
4. `image-4.png` - Elisha’s servant sees only the enemy army from the city wall at dawn and cries out in fear while Elisha remains calm behind him; focus on fear narrowing what he can see.
5. `image-5.png` - Elisha prays and God reveals fiery horses and chariots filling the mountain around them; show that God’s help was already present and greater than the visible danger.
6. `image-6.png` - After being led from Dothan, the blinded Syrians regain sight inside Samaria and discover Israel’s forces around them; show God turning the enemy’s plan into complete helplessness.
7. `image-7.png` - Israel’s king listens to Elisha, lowers his angry response, and serves bread and water to the captured Syrians before sending them home; show mercy bringing peace.
