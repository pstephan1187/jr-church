# The Midnight Basket Escape Image Plan

- **Series:** Adventures in the Bible
- **Lesson:** The Midnight Basket Escape
- **Passage:** Acts 9:19-31; 2 Corinthians 11:32-33
- **Style source:** `../series-image-style.md` and `cover-graphic.png`

1. `image-1.png` - A visibly changed Saul shares a meal with the Damascus believers he once intended to arrest, showing that Jesus gave him a new life and new friends.
2. `image-2.png` - Saul boldly teaches from the Scriptures in a Damascus synagogue while listeners react with amazement, showing that he now proclaims Jesus as the Son of God.
3. `image-3.png` - Hostile leaders and a government official secretly plan Saul's capture over a model or map of Damascus, showing that the former hunter has become the hunted.
4. `image-4.png` - Guards watch and search travelers at the fortified city gates while Saul and a fellow believer observe from a safe hiding place, showing that every ordinary escape route is closed.
5. `image-5.png` - At night, two believers carefully lower Saul in a large woven basket through a window in the city wall while distant guards remain at the gate, showing God's surprising provision through faithful friends.
6. `image-6.png` - Barnabas warmly presents Saul to the cautious Jerusalem believers, with Saul ready to continue preaching, showing that God preserved him and used other Christians to help him keep serving.
